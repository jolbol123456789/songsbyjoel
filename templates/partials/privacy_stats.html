Visitor statistics

To see how many people visit and which songs and links they click, the site counts page views and link clicks with GoatCounter. It uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device. Joel only sees totals, for example “12 clicks on Spotify for a song today”, never who you are.

What is sent: the page or link you clicked, the page title, the site you came from, and a campaign tag if the link you followed had one. Your browser also sends its IP address and browser type, as with any request. GoatCounter uses these briefly to tell unique visits apart and to find your country, and does not store your IP address. GoatCounter’s privacy policy

The legal basis is a legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used (GDPR article 6.1 f). Without JavaScript nothing is counted.
